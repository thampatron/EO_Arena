The Arena

For the needs no labelled dataset covers, experts judge models directly: two anonymous outputs, side by side, and a vote on which is better.

Why an arena

Leaderboards need labels. The domain audit found subdomains with no open labelled data at all, and many datasets whose labels are another model's output. Expert judgement is the reference that remains. Pairwise votes are easier and more consistent to give than absolute scores, and ratings computed from many votes rank models without any ground-truth map. This follows the approach LMArena uses for language models.

How a comparison works

Draw a caseA region, date and task are sampled from an arena, for example thaw slumps on the Tibetan Plateau.
Run two modelsTwo models, chosen at random and kept anonymous, produce outputs from the same inputs.
Expert votesA domain expert sees both over the imagery and picks A, B, a tie, or both unusable.
Update ratingsVotes are pooled into model ratings with uncertainty. Identities are revealed only after the vote.
