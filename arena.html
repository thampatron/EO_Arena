Benchmark ranking

Every benchmark dataset with published GeoFM results, ranked by how much evidence it gives: does it separate models, does it say something the other benchmarks don't, and is there headroom left. Static, from papers; not a model leaderboard.

  • Distinct rankingRanks models unlike the consensus of all other benchmarks (ρ < 0.3). Unique signal, or noise when from one paper.
  • Partly distinctSome shared, some unique signal (0.3 ≤ ρ < 0.7).
  • Tracks consensusRanks models like everything else (ρ ≥ 0.7). Adds little.
  • Saturated / below noiseUnder 3 pts headroom, or model spread under the 2.3-pt cross-paper noise.
  • Under 10 modelsScored, but too few models to judge.

How to read it

Rank orders benchmarks by verdict (distinct, partly distinct, tracks consensus, saturated or below noise, under 10 models), then by the number of models scored. It is a ranking of benchmarks as evidence, not of models. A model leaderboard pooled across papers would mostly measure who wrote each paper: in the same data, OlmoEarth ranks first in 78% of comparisons in its own paper and in 0% of comparisons run by other groups.

Models and papers count distinct models and papers that reported a score on the benchmark. Spread is the median standard deviation of model scores within one paper's comparison (percent metrics only). Headroom is 100 minus the median best score. ρ vs consensus compares the benchmark's model ranking with the mean ranking over all other benchmarks, after z-scoring within each comparison so that paper offsets cancel; it needs at least ten models. Self-reported is the share of comparisons that come from a paper scoring its own model.

Source: a meta-analysis workbook of 14,600 reported scores from 52 GeoFM papers (Viki Mancoridis, 2026, unpublished), grouped into 259 datasets and placed in the EOArena taxonomy. Original results only; copied numbers, ablation variants and non-foundation baselines are dropped. Protocols are pooled within a benchmark. Benchmarks marked not in inventory have published scores but no record yet in the Explorer. Method details on the About page.

These are descriptive statistics, not the latent-factor analysis EOArena is building. A benchmark scored in one paper reflects that paper's protocol and model choice.
