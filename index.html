EOArena · task taxonomy · draft explorer

Browse the audited EO tasks by any facet

118 labelled Earth observation datasets, each tagged on the same 13 facets with a fixed vocabulary. Pick a facet tab, choose values, and see every matching dataset across domains. Values within one facet combine with OR; different facets combine with AND. Switch to the matrix to cross any two facets and see where the empty cells are.

Tags are a first pass from our audit of frameworks, sensors and datasets, and are under review. The ability facet is a hypothesis and is drawn with dashed borders. Dataset facts and sources are on the domain audit. Found an error or a missing dataset? Propose a change.
