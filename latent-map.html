A provisional second capability separates multi-temporal, multi-sensor tasks from high-resolution optical tasks

The latent factor model of the lab’s scoring note describes each GeoFM by a small number of hidden capabilities and each task by its weights on them. With one capability, GeoFMs fall on a single ranking. Allowing a second, the strongest additional pattern in the published scores runs from multi-temporal and SAR + optical tasks such as crop-type mapping, BioMassters and BreizhCrops to high-resolution optical tasks.

Provisional: a hypothesis, not a result

The primary analysis retains one capability (Kfinal = 1). This page shows the K = 2 sensitivity fit. A second capability improves the held-out ordering of GeoFM pairs from 67.2% to 68.6%, but it is not stable when the set of GeoFMs is resampled: the correlation of the task geometry with the full fit is 0.32, against 0.317 at the 95th percentile of shuffled data and below every fixed stability threshold from 0.6 to 0.9. Of the 438 tasks evaluated on at least 9 GeoFMs, 53 have a 90% resampling interval for their capability-2 weight that excludes 0, close to the roughly 10% expected by chance.

Terms

TaskOne dataset evaluated under one adaptation protocol, label fraction, metric and input variant. Scores within a task are comparable across GeoFMs; scores from different tasks are not.
GeoFM familyOne pretraining method with its size and version variants merged, since they share a recipe and would otherwise count the same method several times. Variants pretrained on different imagery are kept as separate families.
Weight on a capabilityHow strongly a task’s published scores follow a GeoFM’s position on that capability (the task’s loading). Capability 1 is the shared ranking; capability 2 is the next pattern once the shared ranking is removed.

Tasks along capability 1: the shared ranking

Capability 1 is the single ranking of GeoFM families that best explains the published scores. The model can be fitted on all tasks, on tasks evaluated with one kind of adaptation, or on one input type or application domain under one adaptation (combinations with at least 20 tasks evaluated on 9 or more GeoFMs); choose the set below. For each task in that set with at least 9 GeoFMs, the view shows how closely the task’s own published ranking agrees with the shared ranking. Each point is one task; its area grows with the number of GeoFMs evaluated on it. Horizontal position: Spearman rank correlation between the task’s published scores and the GeoFMs’ positions on capability 1 (1 = same order, 0 = unrelated, negative = reversed). Black ticks mark group medians. Only tasks in the chosen set are fitted and shown. The shared ranking is fitted on all scores in the set, including each task’s own, so these agreements are optimistic. In every fit, every task’s weight on capability 1 is positive: no task rewards the shared ranking in reverse.

GeoFM families, aggregated by pretraining imagery

GeoFM positions are shown only as group summaries: the median and interquartile interval of capability-2 positions for families with at least 5 tasks, by the imagery they were pretrained on. Groups with fewer than 10 families are omitted.

Family positions and task weights are on different scales; only the sign is comparable. A family with a positive capability-2 position does relatively better on tasks with positive weights. The group medians differ in the same direction as the task groups, but the interquartile intervals overlap substantially.

Method and data

Model. The standardised score of GeoFM family m on task t is modelled as λt⊤θm plus noise, with θm the family’s capabilities and λt the task’s weights (the latent factor model of S. Wang, GeoFM Benchmark Scoring, 2026). Task weights are hierarchical: they are shrunk towards shared group values, with ridge penalty 10. The number of capabilities was chosen by a pre-declared rule (held-out gain and resampling stability); it selects Kfinal = 1. The map shows the K = 2 fit as a sensitivity analysis.

Axes. The two capabilities are rotated to the principal axes of the task weights (each task weighted by the square root of its number of GeoFMs) and signed so that the average weight is positive. Any rotation fits equally well, so read positions relative to one another, not as absolute values. Resampling refits are aligned to the full fit by an orthogonal Procrustes rotation before plotting.

Data. Published scores from the lab’s meta-analysis workbook, Stage 1 core table: 250 GeoFM families × 1,299 tasks, 9,348 scores from 122 papers. No individual scores are shown. Some dataset names are withheld where they coincide with the name of a GeoFM. Data file: data/latent_map_public.json, built by scripts/v5/export_latent_map_public.py.
