Task template v0.2 draft

A dataset becomes a benchmark task once its inputs, target, splits and evaluation are fixed. Every model is then compared on exactly the same thing, through an adapter that must declare what it did.

Rules for every task

Inputs by sensorA dictionary keyed by modality. Each is time × bands × height × width, with a valid mask where true means valid.
Named bandsEvery channel points to the band registry, with wavelengths in µm. Derived and auxiliary layers are declared as such.
Physical unitsStored values are converted with a declared scale and offset. No-data pixels are masked, never passed as numbers.
Time by calendarTime steps are chosen by calendar window, not by position in the series. Every step carries its date.
Pinned dataEach split is a manifest of samples with a checksum per file, at a fixed data version.
Nothing hiddenAdapters declare bands used, how missing bands are filled, how images are resized and which normalisation was applied.
