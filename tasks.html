Task cards

A dataset becomes a benchmark task only once its inputs, outputs and evaluation are fixed. Each card states them exactly, so models are compared on the same thing.

What a card fixes

InputsSensors, bands with wavelengths and resolution, image size, time steps
OutputPrediction type, classes, ignored labels
SplitTrain, validation and test regions; spatial or random
MetricHow a prediction is scored
ProtocolWhat is trained: frozen encoder with a probe, or fine-tuning

The first cards come directly from PANGAEA's published configuration files. Fields those files do not state are marked UNVERIFIED; the split type is the most important of these. Band wavelengths and resolutions come from the stactools band tables for Sentinel-2 and Landsat and from ESA for Sentinel-1.
