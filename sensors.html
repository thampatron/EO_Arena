Open Earth observation sensors

What a benchmark can draw on, as of September 2026: resolution, revisit, status, licence, and how often GeoFM benchmarks use each one.

What stands out

5 satellitesappear in two or more benchmark suites: Sentinel-2, Sentinel-1, Landsat, HLS and MODIS. The only other is French IGN aerial imagery, via FLAIR.
US + Europehold almost all wall-to-wall open sub-metre imagery (Uruguay and South Africa are exceptions). Very-high-resolution tasks elsewhere depend on commercial data.
No globalhyperspectral archive exists. EnMAP and PRISMA are tasked; EMIT is limited to the ISS orbit.
2025–26changed the constellation: Sentinel-1A retired, Sentinel-2C replaced 2A, Landsat 7 ended, NISAR data opened. Suomi NPP data stops 1 Nov 2026.

Free, open and non-commercial

Free is about price. Open and non-commercial are about what you may do with the data, which decides whether a public benchmark can host a task.

OpenAnyone may use, modify and redistribute, including commercially. At most attribution or share-alike.
Free + termsNo cost, but the provider's terms apply: registration, attribution, limits on redistribution.
Non-commercialFree, but commercial use is barred. Companies submitting to a leaderboard may count as commercial use.
RestrictedPaid, quota-based or limited to named users. Needs a data agreement.
