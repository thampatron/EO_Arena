Evidence map

Every benchmark dataset with a reliability score, one scatter plot per domain. Pick the two metrics to plot, colour by subdomain or task family, hover a point for the numbers and click it to open the dataset. Click the i next to an axis for the metric's definition.

    How to read it

    Each point is one benchmark dataset placed by two of the metrics from the Benchmark ranking. Filled points have measured seed noise; hollow points are provisional (noise plugged in from similar comparisons). Point area is proportional to the number of models scored, so a large point is well-supported evidence and a small one is a single paper. Datasets scored in the literature but not yet in the Explorer are included and marked not in inventory in the tooltip. Non-imagery benchmarks (coordinate-only inputs) are left out.

    Useful views. Score vs frontier score shows saturation: points far below the diagonal separate weak models from good ones but not the leading models from each other. Spread vs seed noise is the raw material of the score; the dashed line is spread = noise, where the score is 0.5. Dataset-specific signal vs info share separates benchmarks that measure something of their own from benchmarks that mostly repeat the suite. Models vs score shows how much the score rests on.

    Colours follow the entity, not the rank: a subdomain or task family keeps its colour across every panel and every filter. The table view lists the same points for screen readers and for copying.
