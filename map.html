Where the labels are

Each country is shaded by how many labelled datasets cover it: global datasets count for every country, plus the datasets that name it. Click a country to see them.

Coverage

  • Global datasets are counted for every country. Regional datasets and those with no documented geography are listed beside the map. Global coverage is taken from each dataset's documentation, not from its actual sample locations.
  • Next version: once EOArena hosts task data, the map will plot the location of every sample in each hosted dataset.
  • Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:110m.
