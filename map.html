    How to read this

    • A country counts as labelled when at least one dataset in the chosen domain names it in its documented geography. Global, regional and undocumented datasets cannot be placed on a country, so they are counted beside the map instead.
    • High need means the top 30 countries on the chosen proxy. Proxies are coarse: population for settlements and disasters, arable land for agriculture, forest area for ecosystems. Water, ocean, cryosphere, atmosphere and solid-Earth needs have no country-level proxy yet.
    • Need values are the most recent year the World Bank reports for each country. Boundaries are Natural Earth 1:110m.
