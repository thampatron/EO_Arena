Model ranking

Every geospatial foundation model with published results on imagery benchmarks, ranked by how it does in evaluations run by groups other than its own. Static, from papers. Read the caveats before quoting a rank.

A ranking pooled from papers is only as comparable as the papers are. Scores are z-scored inside each paper's own comparison, so paper and benchmark offsets cancel, but protocols are pooled and most models were evaluated by one or two groups. In this same data the median gap between a model's self-reported and independent z-score is large; the two columns are kept apart for that reason.

How to read it

Independent z is the model's mean z-score over comparisons from papers that did not introduce it. A comparison is one paper's table with at least four models on the same benchmark, protocol, metric and training fraction. z = 0 is the average model in that comparison; z = +1 is one standard deviation better. Win rate is the share of other models in those comparisons that the model beats. Self-reported z is the same statistic over the model's own papers. FT and frozen split the independent z by whether the backbone was fine-tuned or kept frozen (linear probe, kNN, frozen decoder).

Models are ranked by independent z and need at least 10 independent comparisons on at least 5 benchmarks (within a domain or subdomain: 5 comparisons on 2 benchmarks). Models below that threshold are listed unranked. The Scope control switches to a ranking precomputed on one domain or subdomain's benchmarks only; the fewer benchmarks a scope has, the more its ranking reflects one or two papers' choices. Imagery benchmarks only: location-embedding models scored on coordinate-only tasks are left out. Original results only; copied numbers, ablation variants and non-foundation baselines are dropped. general vision marks ImageNet-, web- or CLIP-pretrained backbones included as baselines.

What this ranking is not: a verdict on which model is best. GEO-Bench-2 and PANGAEA show that fine-tuned and frozen rankings disagree, and that no model leads on every task. Use the benchmark ranking to see which tasks separate models at all. Source: a meta-analysis workbook of 14,600 reported scores from 52 GeoFM papers (Viki Mancoridis, 2026, unpublished); per-model aggregates only. Built by scripts/build_model_ranking.py.
